# Hunt Engagement Guide

## Purpose

This records the attack chain for this engagement: initial access, privilege
escalation, domain compromise, and what Sentinel detected at each step. It's
written from the completed engagement so it can be reviewed or reproduced
without relying on chat history.

Nothing is recorded here yet. The methodology and scope are locked in
[ADR-001](../engineering-decisions/001-engagement-methodology.md) and
[ADR-002](../engineering-decisions/002-planted-misconfiguration.md), and
this guide gets filled in as the engagement actually runs.
